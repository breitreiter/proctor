#. Classifier model provider

Proctor supports classifier checks. These checks use a lightweight AI judge to answer questions about a run ("does the answer claim the work is finished?").

These checks are usually significantly faster and cheaper than asking a full chat AI. However, they require specialized models, like Jev or Clef.
