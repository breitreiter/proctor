# Society handbook

## Plots and rent

A full plot is 250 square metres and a half plot 125 square metres. Rent is
£38 a year for a half plot and £70 a year for a full plot, due on 1 October.

## Waiting list

The waiting list is currently 14 months for a half plot and 2 years for a full
plot. Write to the site secretary to join it.

## Water

Hosepipes may be used only before 9 am and after 6 pm. Troughs can be used at any time.

## Bonfires

Bonfires are allowed only in November, and only for dry woody waste. Never
leave a bonfire unattended.

## Sheds and structures

A shed may be at most 6 ft by 4 ft, and no taller than 7 ft. A greenhouse
needs the committee's written agreement before it goes up.

## Keys

Each plot holder gets one gate key. A replacement key costs £15.
