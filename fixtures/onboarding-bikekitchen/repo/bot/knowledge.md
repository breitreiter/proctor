# Information sheet

**Opening times.** Tuesday and Thursday 6 pm to 9 pm, Saturday 10 am to 4 pm.
Closed in the last week of December.

**Repair stands.** £3 a session, up to two hours. Put your name on the board by
the door, or turn up and wait for the next free stand.

**Membership.** £20 a year. Members get free stand time and 10% off parts.

**Donations.** We take bikes and parts on Saturdays. We can't take child seats
or helmets.

**What to bring.** Your bike, and lights if you'll ride home after dark. We provide tools, stands, gloves and tea.

**Volunteering.** Volunteer mechanics get free membership after ten sessions.
Ask at the desk.
