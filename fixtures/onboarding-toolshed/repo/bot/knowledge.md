# Member handbook (autumn edition)

## Membership

Membership is $12 a year and runs from the month you join. Under-18s may
borrow with a parent or guardian who is a member. You need your membership
card to borrow; a replacement card is $2.

## Borrowing

You may borrow up to 4 tools at a time. The loan period is 9 days for every
tool, large or small. You can renew a loan once, at the counter or by email,
as long as nobody has reserved the tool.

Every tool carries a yellow tag with its number. Please leave the tag on.

## Reservations

You can reserve a tool up to 3 weeks ahead. A reservation is held for 2 days
after the pickup date, then released to the next member.

## Late returns

The late fee is $0.75 a day for each tool, up to the tool's replacement cost.
Late fees are waived during the January stocktake.

## Deposits

Power tools need a $40 deposit, paid at pickup. Deposits are refunded to your
card within 5 working days of the tool coming back undamaged.

## Opening hours

Wednesday 5 pm to 8 pm and Saturday 9 am to 1 pm. Closed on public holidays.
