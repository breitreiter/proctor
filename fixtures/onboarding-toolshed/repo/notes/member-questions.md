# What members ask the bot

Collected from the counter and the bot's chat logs, most common first.

- How long can I keep a tool once I've borrowed it?
- What's the late fee if I bring something back late?
- How much does membership cost?
- How far in advance can I reserve a tool?
- I returned the pressure washer on Saturday. When do I get my deposit back?

Someone at the counter said the bot told them the late fee was $7.50 a day.
