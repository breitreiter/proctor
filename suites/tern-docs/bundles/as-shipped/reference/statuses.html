Statuses

A shipment's status is one of these.

StatusMeaning
bookedBooked; not yet collected. It can still be cancelled.
collectedCollected by a Tern driver. It is on its way.
in_transitMoving through Tern's network.
heldAt customs, waiting for clearance. This is normal for parcels to Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands and abroad, clears within two working days, and needs nothing from you: the parcel is still on its way.
deliveredDelivered.
returnedCould not be delivered and is coming back to you. Contact the customer.
cancelledCancelled before collection.
