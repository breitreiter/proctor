Quotes

POST /v2/quotes

Prices for one parcel on every service Tern offers between two postcodes.

FieldType
from_postcodestringWhere the parcel is collected.
to_postcodestringWhere it is delivered.
weightintegerThe packed weight in grams. Up to 20,000.

The response holds quotes, one per service (economy, standard, express), each with an id, its amount in pence, the currency and eta_days. They are in no particular order. A parcel over 20 kg is a 422 with "error": "too_heavy".
