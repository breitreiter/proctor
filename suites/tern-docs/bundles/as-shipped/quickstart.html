Quickstart

Base URL and key

Production is https://api.tern.example. Your sandbox is at the URL in your dashboard; test keys start with tern_test_. Send the key as a bearer token on every request:

Authorization: Bearer tern_test_...

Requests and responses are JSON.

Your first quote

curl -X POST "$TERN_API_URL/v2/quotes" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TERN_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"from_postcode": "EH6 6QT", "to_postcode": "M1 1AE", "weight": 1200}'

Weights are in grams, as a whole number: the parcel above weighs 1.2 kg. Amounts are in pence, as a whole number.
